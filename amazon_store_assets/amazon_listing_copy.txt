HOW IT WORKS:
1. LAUNCH ON YOUR TV OR TABLET: Open Bar Rooms Trivia on your Amazon Fire TV, Fire Stick, or Fire Tablet to display the main game board.

3. REAL-TIME MULTIPLAYER ACTION: When the host starts the round, questions appear simultaneously on the TV screen and players' phones. Answer on your device within the countdown timer, earn points for correct answers, and compete for top leaderboard glory!
