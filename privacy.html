Privacy Policy for Bar Rooms Trivia

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

Welcome to Bar Rooms Trivia. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data collected through your use of our mobile application and web platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate live trivia games:

  • Account Information: If you sign up or log in using Email or Google Sign-In, we store your email address and basic profile identifier via Supabase Authentication.
  • Gameplay Data: Game session nickname, room code, and live answer responses. Anonymous players join without providing an email address.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Authenticate game hosts and track session history.
  • Calculate live scores, display leaderboard standings, and process game rounds.
  • Improve application performance and fix technical issues.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We utilize trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: Cloud database and authentication services.
  • Google OAuth: Optional single sign-on service for hosts.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Hosts may request deletion of their account and associated data at any time by contacting support.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@barroomstrivia.com
